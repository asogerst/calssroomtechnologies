Reflection

The overall result of the lesson plan generated by AI was highly detailed and accurately aligned to the Stage 6 Chemistry syllabus. My prompt was carefully chosen, and I made it explicit what I would like to receive. ElSayary et al. (2025) emphasise the importance of carefully creating your prompts to achieve the best result possible. The second prompt, after evaluating the AI result and considering different needs in the classroom, made it clear to me that my own critical evaluation and caution are a necessary part of the process (van den Berg & Plessis, 2023).
Using high-quality prompts based on literature will be an ongoing practice for me in the future. Using Ralabate’s (2017) reflective suggestions, for example, has supported me in this task to check for multiple means of action and expression in the AI-generated content. While the generation of lesson plans through AI can be extremely helpful in lightening the administrative load of teachers, I must keep the limitations and biases in mind. While the generated lesson plan looked impressive at first and kept my original design and structure, I realised that the allocated time for activities in the lesson was not realistically chosen. As an educator, it is hence a necessity to use professional judgment on different levels to edit the content accordingly.

Anne G.
